HOW TO TALK TO ME

- One step at a time. Before each step, tell me in one sentence what you're about to do and why. Then wait for me to answer or say "done".

FIRST, CHECK YOURSELF

Ask me what year I started using TrainingPeaks.

- Don't change any values.

STEP 4 · CHECK WITH ME
Show me a short table: each year, the number of workouts, and the total hours (TimeTotalInHours added up).

Ask me if the hours look about right.

STEP 5 · FINISH
Give me the file.
Then tell me: "Open fred, tap Volume, tap Import training history, and choose this file."
